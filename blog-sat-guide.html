1. Understanding the Digital SAT in 2026

The SAT went fully digital in March 2024 and the 2026 version is a refined, adaptive test that rewards preparation more than ever. Here's what you're dealing with:

  • Total Time: 2 hours 14 minutes (down from 3+ hours)
  • Format: 2 sections — Reading & Writing, then Math
  • Adaptive: Module 2 difficulty depends on your Module 1 performance
  • Score Range: 400–1600 (200–800 per section)
  • Calculator: Allowed throughout the entire Math section

⭐ Key Insight

The adaptive format means if you perform well in Module 1, you'll get harder questions in Module 2 — but harder questions are worth more points. Always aim for the hard module.

3. The Ednivo 8-Week Study Plan

This is the exact schedule our students follow. It's built around 2 hours of focused study per day — achievable even for students in school full-time.

Week 1–2: Diagnostic & Foundation

Start with a full-length diagnostic test under timed conditions. Don't study first — you need a true baseline. After the test, categorise every wrong answer into three buckets: Careless Mistakes, Concept Gaps, and Time Issues.

Week 3–4: Math Deep Dive

Math accounts for 800 of your 1600 points — making it the highest-leverage section. Focus on the following topic clusters in order of SAT frequency:

  1. Linear equations & inequalities — ~25% of Math questions
  2. Data analysis & statistics — ~20% of questions
  3. Advanced algebra (quadratics, functions) — ~25%
  4. Geometry & trigonometry — ~15%
  5. Word problems & rates — ~15%

Week 5–6: Reading & Writing Strategy

The Reading & Writing section rewards active reading and precise vocabulary. The most effective strategies:

  • Read the question before the passage — know what you're looking for
  • Annotate as you read — circle main claims, underline evidence, mark tone shifts
  • Eliminate clearly wrong answers first — answers with "always," "never," "only" are usually traps
  • For Transition questions — identify the logical relationship (contrast, continuation, cause-effect) before looking at options

Week 7–8: Mock Tests & Final Sprint

Take at least 2 full-length mock tests in real exam conditions. Review every wrong answer. Focus the final week on your 2 weakest sub-topics only — not general review.

⚡ Pro Tip

The night before your SAT: do zero studying. Sleep 8 hours. Eat a real breakfast. Arrive 20 minutes early. Your brain is already prepared — trust the process.

5. The 10 Mistakes That Kill Your SAT Score

  1. Studying without a diagnostic baseline
  2. Doing practice questions without reviewing wrong answers
  3. Only practising easy questions to build confidence
  4. Ignoring time management strategy
  5. Not taking full-length mocks under real conditions
  6. Memorising vocabulary lists instead of learning roots
  7. Skipping the hardest Math topics because they feel intimidating
  8. Over-studying Reading and under-studying Math
  9. Cramming the week before the test
  10. Not knowing the format and structure before test day
