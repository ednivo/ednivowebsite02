2. USD Loans vs INR Loans: The Hidden Currency Risk

One of the most consequential decisions for students is whether to borrow in US Dollars (Prodigy, MPOWER) or Indian Rupees (HDFC Credila, SBI):

  • USD Loans (Prodigy / MPOWER): Ideal if you are 100% committed to working in the US on STEM OPT or H-1B visas. Because you earn in dollars and repay in dollars, you eliminate foreign exchange risk. However, if you return to India, currency depreciation can inflate your INR debt service.
  • INR Loans (HDFC Credila / Indian Banks): Borrow in INR with fixed currency liability. Your parents can claim Section 80E tax deductions on interest paid for up to 8 years.

💡 Tax Advantage

For families in the 30% tax slab, Indian bank loans under Section 80E can effectively reduce the real interest cost by 2.5% to 3.5% annually.

3. Eligibility and Target Universities

  1. Prodigy Finance: Evaluates your future earning potential rather than your parents' credit score. Supports over 850 top business, engineering, and law schools worldwide.
  2. MPOWER Financing: Supports undergraduate and graduate students at over 400 approved US and Canadian colleges, offering fixed interest rates.
  3. HDFC Credila: Broadest university coverage with custom disbursement schedules for living expenses, health insurance, and tuition fees.

4. TCS (Tax Collected at Source) & LRS Rules

Under India's Liberalized Remittance Scheme (LRS), education remittances funded by an education loan from an approved financial institution are subject to a nominal TCS of 0.5% for amounts exceeding ₹7 Lakhs, compared to 5% for self-funded remittances.
