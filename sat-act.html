Cohort Structure

How the 16-Class
Cohort Works

Each cohort is a complete, structured journey from diagnostic to mastery — delivered fully online.

🎯

Classes 1–4: Diagnostic & Foundations

We start with a full diagnostic test to identify your strengths and weak areas. The first 4 classes build a rock-solid foundation in Math, Reading, and Writing.

📈

Classes 5–10: Section Mastery

Deep dives into each SAT/ACT section. Timed practice, strategy frameworks, and question-type breakdowns to systematically improve your score.

🔥

Classes 11–14: Full Mock Tests

Back-to-back full-length mock tests under real exam conditions. Detailed score analysis after every test to track your improvement.

🏁

Classes 15–16: Final Sprint

Last-minute tips, exam-day strategy, mental preparation, and a final mock. You leave confident and fully prepared for test day.
