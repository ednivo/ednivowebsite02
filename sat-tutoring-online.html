A practical study method

How to prepare for the digital SAT online

Use a repeatable cycle: assess, learn, practise and review. Adapt the time you spend on each step to your own results and schedule.

Check your starting point

Take a diagnostic or timed practice test and note the skills that need the most attention.

Build a weekly plan

Set manageable sessions for concepts, targeted questions and review, with time for both test sections.

Practise under time

Mix topic drills with timed sets. Practise choosing efficient methods without rushing accuracy.

Review every mistake

Track why an answer went wrong, revisit the concept and try similar questions to check improvement.

Two sections, one plan

Prepare for SAT Math and Reading and Writing

The digital SAT tests different skills across two scored sections. A balanced study plan should include learning, practice and review for each section, while giving extra time to the areas where you can improve most.

  • Math: algebra, advanced math, problem-solving and data analysis, geometry and trigonometry.
  • Reading and Writing: information and ideas, craft and structure, expression of ideas and standard English conventions.
  • Test strategy: timing, careful reading, calculator fluency where appropriate and reviewing answer choices.

Make practice part of your routine

Short, consistent practice sessions can help you spot recurring mistakes. After each set, review your reasoning rather than only checking whether the answer was right.

  • Practise one skill at a time.
  • Record patterns in incorrect answers.
  • Repeat similar questions after reviewing.
  • Use timed mocks to practise stamina and pacing.

Online SAT prep FAQs

Answers to common questions about online tutoring, courses and practice.

Can I prepare for the SAT online?

Yes. Online preparation can combine live lessons, independent practice, timed tests and feedback. Ednivo offers structured live group preparation and personalised 1-on-1 tutoring.

What does Ednivo SAT tutoring cover?

Preparation covers the digital SAT Math and Reading and Writing sections, with concept teaching, practice questions, assignments, mock tests and performance review.

Should I choose group classes or 1-on-1 SAT tutoring?

A live group course can suit students who want a structured schedule and cohort learning. 1-on-1 tutoring can suit students who want a more individualised plan or focused help in selected areas. Talk to Ednivo to discuss your needs.

How should I start preparing for the digital SAT?

Start by taking a diagnostic or timed practice test, review your performance by skill, set a realistic target and build a weekly plan that includes learning, practice and review. You can also explore the score calculator and SAT syllabus.

Can I practise SAT questions online?

Yes. Use high-quality digital SAT practice questions and timed sets, then review every answer to understand both the correct method and any recurring mistakes. Visit Ednivo's practice area to explore available resources.
