How to use this SAT syllabus

The digital SAT measures a defined set of Reading and Writing and Math skills. Use the domains below as a checklist, then pair each topic with practice questions and a review of your mistakes. The official College Board guide is the source to consult for the current test structure and policies.

Reading and Writing study priorities

Practise finding evidence in short passages, interpreting words in context, evaluating arguments, choosing logical transitions, and editing grammar and punctuation. After each set, note whether an error came from comprehension, vocabulary, reasoning or a language rule.

Math study priorities

Review linear equations, systems, functions, nonlinear expressions, ratios, percentages, data interpretation, geometry and trigonometry. Practise translating word problems into equations and checking whether your answer is reasonable.

Build a repeatable weekly routine

  • Start with a diagnostic or timed set to identify the weakest skills.
  • Study one or two focused topics at a time, then complete targeted questions.
  • Keep an error log that records the reason for each missed question.
  • Repeat similar questions later and use full-length practice tests to develop timing.
