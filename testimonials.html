The 5-Part Testimonial Script

Use this script as a guide — don't memorise it word-for-word. Speak naturally. 2 minutes is the perfect length. Record on your phone in good lighting.

01
Introduction (15 seconds)
Prompt: Who are you and where are you from?
"Hi, my name is [NAME]. I'm [AGE] years old and I'm from [CITY, COUNTRY]. I'm currently applying to universities in the United States with a focus on [MAJOR]."
02
The Problem (20 seconds)
Prompt: What was your situation before Ednivo?
"Before Ednivo, my SAT score was [STARTING SCORE] and I had no clear plan. I was overwhelmed by the preparation process and didn't know where to start. I'd tried [other resource/method] but it wasn't working for me."
03
The Solution (30 seconds)
Prompt: What did Ednivo do for you specifically?
"Then I joined Ednivo. What made the difference was [specific thing — e.g. the 16-class structure / my counsellor / the mock tests / the affordable price]. My counsellor [NAME] was [describe them]. The classes were [live/recorded/structured] and I could [specific benefit — ask questions/rewatch/practice]."
04
The Result (20 seconds)
Prompt: What score did you achieve? What did it lead to?
"After [X weeks] with Ednivo, my SAT score went from [OLD SCORE] to [NEW SCORE] — an improvement of [POINTS] points. I've now been accepted to [UNIVERSITY] and I'm [enrolled / starting in Fall 2026]."
05
The Recommendation (15 seconds)
Prompt: What would you say to someone considering Ednivo?
"If you're thinking about Ednivo — just do it. The price is unbeatable, the quality is genuinely world-class, and the support is unlike anything else I found. My only regret is that I didn't start sooner."

💡 Recording Tips

Film in landscape mode. Use natural light (face a window). Hold the phone steady or prop it up. Speak clearly and at a normal pace. One take is fine — authenticity beats perfection.

📍 Background

A clean wall or bookshelf works perfectly. Avoid busy backgrounds. If filming from your bedroom, tidy it first. Your face should be well-lit, not backlit.

🎵 Audio

Record in a quiet room. Close windows. Turn off fans. Speak directly toward your phone microphone. Test the audio with a 10-second clip first.

⏱️ Length

2 minutes is ideal. 90 seconds is fine. Under 60 seconds is too short. Over 3 minutes is too long. Don't overthink it — just answer the 5 prompts naturally.
